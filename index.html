How To Play

Move through the house with Move Forward / Move Back or swipe up/down. Enter / Use Room interacts with the current room. Knock / Door Action is for doors and bedrooms.

Sounds are clues. Crying, coughing, vomiting, footsteps, doors, school bells, washing machine, vacuum, and playground ambience all mean something is happening.

The game does not always tell you what is wrong. You investigate by checking children, carrying items, and trying care actions.

Physical care uses movement, carrying, and objects. Conversations use typed replies.

If a child refuses food, drink or a bottle, that probably was not the problem.
